Lossless, not streamed

These are FLAC files — the same data as a CD. A streaming service sends about 1 MB a minute; these are roughly 7 MB a minute.

The whole song downloads first. That's the wait. Once it starts, it can't stutter — and it's kept on disk afterwards, so it never downloads twice.
It gets faster. The next track downloads quietly while you listen.
Titles and covers come from the files. Each track's own tags and embedded artwork. If a cover looks wrong, tap the pencil beside it.
Files live on Google Drive, which is storage rather than a music service — it can be slow when several of us are listening. If a track fails, wait a moment and press play again.
Mind your data. One song is 25–70 MB.
