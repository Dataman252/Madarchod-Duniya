Lossless, not streamed

These are FLAC files — the same data as a CD, nothing discarded. A normal streaming service sends roughly 1 MB per minute. These are about 7 MB per minute.

The whole song downloads first. Rather than trickling audio in as you listen, the entire file is fetched up front. That's the wait — but once it starts it won't stutter or cut out.
It gets faster. While you listen, the next track downloads quietly in the background. Anything you've played stays in memory, so replaying is instant.
Titles and covers come from the files themselves. Each track's own tags and embedded artwork are read directly. If a cover looks wrong, tap the pencil beside it to pick a better one.
Files live on Google Drive. Drive is built for storage, not for serving music to many people at once, so it can be slow when several of us are here. If a track fails, wait a moment and press play again.
Mind your data. One song is 25–70 MB. Wi-Fi is a good idea.
