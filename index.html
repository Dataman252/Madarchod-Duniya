Lossless, not streamed

This page plays FLAC files — the same data that's on a CD, with nothing thrown away. A normal streaming service sends you a compressed version at around 1 MB per minute. These are roughly 7 MB per minute.

The whole song downloads first. Rather than trickling audio in while you listen, the entire file is fetched up front. That's why there's a wait — but once it starts, it won't stutter or cut out partway through.
It gets faster. While you're listening, the next track quietly downloads in the background. Songs you've already played stay in memory, so replaying one is instant.
Files live on Google Drive. Drive is built for storage, not for serving music to lots of people at once, so it can be slow when several listeners are here together. If a track fails, wait a moment and press play again.
Watch your data. One song is 25–70 MB. On mobile data that adds up quickly — Wi-Fi is a good idea.
